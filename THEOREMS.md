# No.86 — Exact shortest reconfiguration distance in CNF formulas parameterized by number of clauses
Version 1.0 publication candidate — 2026-11-25. Ryutaro Yonezu. NOT HUMAN PEER REVIEWED; NOVELTY INCONCLUSIVE; REFERENCES PARTIAL. All rights reserved.

## Scope
Let F be a CNF with n Boolean variables and m clauses; s,t are satisfying assignments. The solution graph has satisfying assignments as vertices and single-bit flips as edges. Distance is infinity if disconnected. Delete tautological clauses and duplicate literals, and complement coordinates to normalize s=0. Write D for variables with t=1 and S for the rest. Empty clause cannot occur for a satisfiable input. All proofs concern normalized formulas. In particular every remaining clause has a negative literal because s=0 is satisfying. The implementation expects these conditions (it is a finite verification harness, not an unrestricted CNF parser). The claim is a theoretical bound, not a measured asymptotic speedup.

## 1. Twin reduction
Partition variables by their signed clause incidence and endpoint bit. Keep min(q,2) representatives per class of size q. The reduced formula drops all other literals. Then d_F = d_reduced + sum_{classes with t=1} max(q-2,0), including infinity.
Proof: For each class q>=2 the clause contributions depend only on whether its count of ones is 0, strictly between 0 and q, or q. Map these to 00,10,11 of two representatives; leave singleton classes unchanged. Every original flip maps to a reduced flip or stutter. Each changing class must cross its q-2 internal mixed-state transitions at least once. This proves the lower bound and projects every path. Conversely, in a reduced path replace each nonsingleton class's local trajectory between first entering and last leaving mixed state by one fixed mixed state (choose the bit set at first entry). This can only increase clause satisfaction, because mixed supplies both signs; it never adds flips. Classes never entering mixed remain constant. Lift each same-endpoint mixed excursion to one original bit turned on then off; lift each changing class to first one bit on, then q-2 further bits on while mixed, then the last bit on at the prescribed exit. The internal additions do not alter clause contributions. Singleton flips are unchanged. This proves the upper bound. Unreachable cases follow from the two path constructions. The three-state mapping for q=2 may coalesce the two mixed patterns, which is harmless because they have equal clause contributions. The two-representative threshold is sharp for literal deletion: (x1 OR x2 OR NOT y) AND (NOT x1 OR NOT x2 OR y) allows 000 -> 100 -> 101 -> 111, whereas deleting x2 leaves x1=y and disconnects 00 from 11.

## 2. Matching core
Build a bipartite graph with four roles per clause: positive D, negative D, positive S, negative S. Each role is adjacent to variables of that category appearing in that clause. Take a maximum matching M. Start from all unmatched roles; close under role-to-all-neighbors and variable-to-its-matched-role. Let A be reached roles and K reached variables. Every reached variable is matched, otherwise an augmenting path exists (traversing a matched edge forward only returns to its source role). All reached variables have their matched role in A; every matched role in A has its matched variable in K. Every role with a neighbor outside K is outside A and is matched to a variable outside K. Its representative is distinct from all other roles' representatives. In particular |K|<=4m; at least |K| roles are matched inside A.

## 3. Eliminate changing variables outside K
Let E=D\\K, W=S\\K. Let H be F with all clauses containing a variable in E deleted; the remaining formula is on K union W. Claim d_F=|E|+d_H, where H distance uses the restricted endpoints. Projection of any original path gives the lower bound (at least |E| flips of E). For lifting, designate as early the matched positive-D representative for every deleted clause having a positive E literal. By Section 2, each designated positive-D representative belongs to E (it is outside K and belongs to D). All other E variables are late. Every negative-E representative is late by distinctness. Starting at zero, flip early variables; follow an arbitrary H path on K union W; then flip late variables. For a deleted clause with negative E, a late negative representative guarantees it until the late phase. If it has no negative E, early flips cannot destroy its initial satisfaction. During the middle phase either its early positive or late negative representative is true. During the late phase a clause with positive E is already guaranteed by its early representative; a clause with only negative E is guaranteed by its non-E literals at the final assignment, because all E are one there and non-E variables are already final. This proves the claim including connectivity. No formula on K alone is asserted at this step.

## 4. Lower/upper bound and temporary-flip budget
Let H0 delete from H all clauses mentioning W; let L=d_H0 on K. Let g be the number of clauses in H with positive W literals. These g roles are matched outside K, so |K|+g<=4m. Projection implies d_H>=L. If L is infinite, H is disconnected. Otherwise choose the distinct matched positive-S representatives of those g clauses and keep all matched negative-S representatives at zero. Flip the g chosen variables before a shortest H0 path, and flip them back afterwards. In the initial phase, clauses with negative W have a permanent zero representative; clauses without negative W cannot lose satisfaction from the initial flips. During the core phase every removed clause has either a positive representative at one or a negative representative at zero. During the final phase, clauses with negative W still have a zero representative, and clauses with only positive W are guaranteed by the final K values (W=0 at the endpoint). Thus L<=d_H<=L+2g. Any shortest H path projects to a K path in H0 with at least L flips and must use at least two flips of each W variable ever changed; it therefore changes at most g distinct W variables. In particular reachability is decidable in 2^{O(m)} poly(n,m) time.

## 5. Exact distance via clause-mask DP
Index clauses of H. For v in W define N(v),P(v) as clause sets where v occurs negatively/positively. Guess B, the set of clauses containing at least one negative literal of a W variable never flipped in an optimal path. It suffices to enumerate subsets of the union of N(v). Write R=clauses(H)\\B and U={v in W:N(v) intersects R}. For the correct guess every U variable must flip, so |U|<=g. Skip guesses exceeding this bound.
For each v in W\\U choose either inactive (cost 0; cover N(v) intersect B) or active (cost 1; cover P(v) intersect R). A standard union-mask DP keeps minimum cost and one chosen set A for each exact combined coverage mask C; cost above g may be discarded. Require B subset C. For each such mask, drop all covered clauses, and run BFS on K union U in the remaining formula, obtaining d. The candidate is |E|+2|A|+d. Take the minimum of these candidates and the explicit upper bound in Section 4 (plus |E|).

Soundness: Every inactive variable outside K union U remains zero, giving permanent witnesses in B. Every active variable outside K union U has no negative occurrence in R by definition of U. Flip active variables on before the residual BFS, and off afterwards. During initial activation no clause of R can lose truth, and B is always satisfied. During residual BFS covered clauses in R have a positive active witness; uncovered clauses in R have no negative literals outside K union U and no positive literals from active variables, so their truth is exactly the reduced truth. During deactivation each R clause is guaranteed by the final K union U assignment, since all outside variables occur only positively in R and are zero in the endpoint. Add the early/late E phases using Section 3. Thus every finite candidate has a valid original path of the claimed length.

Completeness: Choose a shortest H path and the corresponding B as above. U is contained in its set of ever-flipped W variables. Choose active A to be all other ever-flipped W variables. Every B clause has an inactive negative witness; this choice is represented by the DP and costs at most g. Project the optimal path onto K union U. Every uncovered clause in R contains no negative literal from W\\U and no positive literal from active A, and the inactive variables were always zero. Therefore this projection is a path in the reduced formula. Its length is at most d_H-2|A|. The DP can replace A by an equal-or-cheaper set with identical coverage without changing the reduced formula or invalidating soundness. Hence the minimum candidate is at most d_F. Together with soundness and the upper bound, equality follows. If no path exists, Section 4 detects infinity.

Complexity: at most 2^m guesses B, 2^m masks per guess, DP processing polynomial in n,m per mask, and BFS at most 2^{|K|+|U|}<=2^{|K|+g}<=2^{4m} vertices per mask. Total 2^{6m} poly(n,m) time and 2^{O(m)} poly(n,m) space. Matching, masks, endpoint normalization and reconstruction have polynomial overhead in the stated representation. A polynomial-time maximum matching algorithm can be used in theory; Python's recursive augmenting implementation is only a finite-test implementation. No claim of a polynomial kernel for the whole optimization problem, nor of a tight exponent, is made.

## 7. Related work and scope of contribution
The shortest satisfying-assignment reconfiguration problem is established in the literature. Mouawad, Nishimura, Pathak and Raman [MNPR] classify shortest-path complexity for fixed Boolean relation languages. Their work also handles paths changing variables that agree at the endpoints. The publisher abstract and accessible text excerpts were checked in this revision; direct arXiv PDF/HTML retrieval failed. No assertion of exhaustive comparison with that paper is made.

Matching methods for CNF satisfiability and deficiency are established (Szeider [Sz1], [Sz2]). In [Sz2], pp. 2–3, the ordinary variable-clause incidence graph ignores literal polarities and a clause-covering matching certifies satisfiability. The same discussion describes reduction under assignments for bounded maximum deficiency. This does not by itself establish shortest reconfiguration distance. For example, (x OR NOT y) AND (NOT x OR y) has a clause-covering matching but its two satisfying assignments are disconnected, whereas deleting both clauses makes them connected. This example distinguishes the guarantees, not all possible variants of matching autarky.

Twin compression already implies FPT in m: after normalization, the signed incidence and target bit give at most 2*3^m classes, and at most 4*3^m retained variables. Thus generic FPT is not the main novelty claim. The candidate contribution is the endpoint-sensitive four-role construction, exact additive elimination, temporary-flip budget and coverage DP yielding a single-exponential exact-distance bound 2^{6m} poly(n,m).
